# Stupid mod
Just a stupid mod

## General changes
Block breaking is 60x faster

Totems and shields don't work

Sleeping in the nether vs overworld is inverted

Every advancement or recipe unlock has a 1 in 50 chance to
 - 60x lightning
 - 100 block radius explosion with fire
 - and enable keepInventory to prevent annoyance

Jumping gives you saturation and health

XP bottles explode

## Bedrock stuff

Bedrock can now be mined and eaten

If you put 8 bedrock and a crafting table in a grindstone you get a bedrock workstation

The bedrock workstation can be used to upgrade items
 - Leather armor gets converted to bedrock armor (chestplate and leggings are unfinished so it gives you netherite)
 - Wooden sword and pickaxe gets converted ino bedrock sword and pickaxe
 - 8 TNT gets converted into a nuke
 - 1 Nuke can be converted into throwable nuke and back
 - Ender pearl gets converted into bedrock ender pearl
 - Redstone gets converted into a bedrock accelerator
 - Netherrack gets converted into a bedrock magnet
 - A netherite block gets converted into a bedrock command line
 - Cobblestone gets converted into a duper
 - Bedrock gets converted into a barrier block
 - Coal gets converted into C4
 - An iron ingot gets converted into a C4 Detonator
 - C4 gets converted into C4 machine gun
 - A grass block gets converted into a zero-friction grass block
 - A dirt block gets converted into a falling grass block
 - A stone block gets converted into a fake stone block

Many of these items have special abilities
 - Bedrock helmet gives you night vision
 - Bedrock boots allow you to fly
 - Bedrock sword has a right click ability: Spawns a grid of swords
 - Bedrock pickaxe has a right click ability: Spawns a pickaxe projectile that mines in a 5 block radius
 - Bedrock ender pearl travels straight
 - Bedrock accelerator has a right click ability: Accelerates ticks of blocks when you click on them
 - Bedrock magnet attracts minerals (diamond, ancient debris, etc.) when in mainhand or offhand
 - Bedrock command line gives you operator permissions

## C4

Right click to throw C4

Punch to pick up (if you own it)

Right click to disable camo, shift right click to enable

C4 machine gun can be held down to throw C4 continuously.
You can hold shift to spawn 100 at a time

All of these work in dispensers :)
You can use gunpowder in a dispenser to disarm
